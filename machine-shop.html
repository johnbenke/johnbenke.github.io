Machine Shop Manufacturing Training

MEMS 1001 Machine Shop Practices · Fall 2023

Six graded machining projects on manual equipment, each formally evaluated for tolerance and specification compliance. What this course changed is how I dimension a part — tolerances stopped being numbers on a drawing and became something I had to physically hit, because I have been the person who has to make it.

Process & capability

  • Operated manual engine lathes for turning between centers and in a three-jaw chuck, knurling, grooving, form turning and single-point threading.
  • Operated vertical milling machines using end mills, edge-finder and dial-indicator alignment, and vise fixturing; operated drill presses for drilling, boring, reaming and tapping.
  • Completed conversational CNC programming assignments on two of the six projects and used digital readout (DRO) systems for precision positioning on lathes and mills.
  • Used precision hand tools and inspection equipment throughout: height gage, scriber, edge finder, dial indicator, deburring and engraving tools, tap and tap handle, drill chuck, live center.
  • Manual engine lathe
  • Vertical mill
  • Drill press
  • Conversational CNC
  • DRO
  • Precision measurement
