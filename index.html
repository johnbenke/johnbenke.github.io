Foosball Table — 22-Component Assembly

Coursework · Individual

A 22-component assembly built as a GD&T exercise. Every mating part carries datums and feature control frames, and the fits between rods, bearings, and bushings were specified rather than assumed — the point of the project being that the tolerances, not the nominal dimensions, decide whether it moves.
